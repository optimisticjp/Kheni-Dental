# Copy Style Guide

## Voice

Warm, reassuring, clear, clinically responsible and confident without overselling.

## Writing rules

- Start with the patient's real concern or desired outcome.
- Use short sentences when the topic is stressful or technical.
- Explain clinical terms in everyday language.
- Use treatment names for clarity and SEO, not as the emotional headline every time.
- Do not use em dashes.
- Avoid generic phrases such as "world-class", "state-of-the-art", "transform your life", "unparalleled care" and "cutting-edge solutions" unless a specific factual claim supports them.
- Avoid "best dentist", "No. 1", "100% painless", "guaranteed", "permanent forever" and similar claims.
- Do not invent qualifications, memberships, technologies, awards, patient counts or case counts.
- Never imply that a website page can diagnose a patient.

## Core brand language

Useful themes:

- comfort
- clarity
- confidence
- relief
- trust
- care
- precision
- restore
- understand your options
- feel comfortable asking questions

## CTA language

Prefer:

- Book a consultation
- Ask us on WhatsApp
- Tell us what is bothering you
- Explore your options
- Find a branch
- Plan an international enquiry

Avoid using "Book Now" everywhere.

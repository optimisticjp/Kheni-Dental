## Why gold needs four weights

`#e1af4a` is the site's gold. It reaches 9.87:1 on near-black, which is
excellent, and 1.90:1 on ivory, which is unusable. One gold cannot carry
both jobs, so the work is split:

| Token | Value | Job |
|---|---|---|
| `--brand-gold` | #e1af4a | dark surfaces, fills, stars, the primary button |
| `--brand-gold-text` | #886627 | small text, links and eyebrows on light surfaces |
| `--brand-gold-soft` | #eace8c | hover on dark, hairlines |
| `--brand-gold-tint` | #f8f1df | the lightest gold field |
| `--brand-gold-deep` | #b47825 | bronze, for a rule or an underline |

The logo's rose (`#9b6665`) is not part of this system. It appears only in
the K of the mark, where it is artwork rather than text.
